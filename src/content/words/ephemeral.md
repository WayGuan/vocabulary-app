---
word: ephemeral
type: adjective
phonetic: /ɪˈfem.ər.əl/
definition: Lasting for a very short time; transitory.
example: "The ephemeral beauty of cherry blossoms makes them all the more precious."
tags: [time, nature]
---

**Origin:** Greek *ephemeros* (lasting a day) — *epi* (on) + *hemera* (day)

**Related:** transient, fleeting, momentary, short-lived

**Usage notes:** Often used to describe things of natural beauty or digital content that disappears quickly (e.g., "ephemeral stories" on social media).
