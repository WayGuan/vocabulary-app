---
word: serendipity
type: noun
phonetic: /ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ɪ.ti/
definition: The occurrence of events by chance in a happy or beneficial way.
example: "It was pure serendipity that they met at the bookstore and fell in love."
tags: [luck, discovery]
---

**Origin:** Coined by Horace Walpole in 1754, from the Persian fairy tale *The Three Princes of Serendip* (old name for Sri Lanka).

**Related:** fortune, luck, happy accident, chance, fortuitous

**Usage notes:** One of the most difficult English words to translate. Implies a fortunate accident — not just luck, but a discovery made while looking for something else.
