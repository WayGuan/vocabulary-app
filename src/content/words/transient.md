---
word: transient
type: adjective / noun
phonetic: /ˈtræn.zi.ənt/
definition: Lasting only for a short time; impermanent. As a noun, a person staying somewhere temporarily.
example: "The city's transient population swells during the summer festival."
tags: [time]
---

**Origin:** Latin *transire* — to go across

**Related:** ephemeral, fleeting, temporary, passing

**Usage notes:** As an adjective modifies impermanence; as a noun (somewhat formal) refers to someone without a fixed home.
